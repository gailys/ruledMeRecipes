From sweet to savory, our recipes have mornings covered

Eggs are the backbone of most keto breakfast recipes: scrambles, omelets, frittatas and egg cups. Pair your favorites with sausage, bacon, or a hash built on cauliflower instead of potato. On the sweet side, almond and coconut flour go where wheat used to: pancakes, waffles, muffins, and sliceable loaves still work. Sweeteners like erythritol and allulose fill in for sugar. Chia pudding, yogurt bowls and smoothies cover the mornings where you don't want to cook.

The carbs at breakfast almost never come from eggs. They typically come from sugar-laden yogurt products, flavored coffee creamers, and grain-based cereals. We replace that with a Greek yogurt plus berries, heavy cream or nut milks, and homemade granola; keeping your plate at about a 5g net carb median. If you're not a fan of eggs (or just want a break), we have plenty of recipes that will cover you, too.
